About this calculator

Converted as per ASTM D1250 / IP 200 Petroleum Measurement Table 53B (refined products, glass hydrometer) — the same table used in the oil company density chart. Takes readings of 700–780 kg/m³ for petrol and 790–880 kg/m³ for diesel (g/ml such as 0.7455 also works), at 0–55°C.
